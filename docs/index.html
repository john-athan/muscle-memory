Muscle Memory

Figma, in a browser, behaving like it knows it is in one.

The one that costs you work

⌘⇧R in Figma is Paste to Replace. Press it meaning hard reload, with a slide selected, and Figma swaps that slide for your clipboard.

Left to Figma on purpose: ⌘D ⌘G ⌘⇧G ⌘= ⌘0. On a canvas those read as duplicate, group, ungroup and zoom. The rule is not that the browser always wins. It is that the expected meaning wins.

What it will not promise

  • Moving an action means pressing the old chord for you, with a synthetic event Figma may ignore. The extension measures whether it worked and tells you.
  • Reclaiming a chord has no such risk, which is why the destructive rule uses none.
  • The banner-hiding patterns will rot as Figma redesigns. They fail by doing nothing.
