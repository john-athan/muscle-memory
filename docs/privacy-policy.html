Privacy policy

Muscle Memory collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no server to send it to.

What it stores

Two things, both on your own machine, both through the browser's extension storage:

  • Your settings, in chrome.storage.sync, so they follow your Chrome profile between machines the way your bookmarks do.
  • Two counters, in chrome.storage.local: how many times the extension pressed a shortcut on your behalf, and how many of those Figma acted on. This is what the diagnostics panel reports. It records counts, never keystrokes.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting, no unique identifier.
  • No network requests of any kind. The extension declares no host permissions and cannot reach a remote server.
  • No reading of your Figma files, designs, comments or account. It inspects keyboard and mouse events, and the wording of banners, and nothing else.
  • Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing leaves your machine.

Permissions

The extension requests one permission, storage, for the settings above. Its content script runs only on figma.com, which is declared in the manifest rather than requested as a broad host permission.

Removing your data

"Reset everything" in the options page clears the settings. Uninstalling the extension removes both stores entirely. There is no copy anywhere else.

Verifying all of this

The extension has no build step: the source on GitHub is byte for byte what runs in your browser. Searching it for fetch, XMLHttpRequest or WebSocket returns nothing.
