Banners

Found by the words in the banner, not by how it is built. When Figma redesigns, the worst that happens is a banner comes back. Only ever on an open file, never on the rest of figma.com.

Do the moved shortcuts work?

Three shortcuts had to move. Making them work means pressing the old key for you, and Figma is allowed to ignore a keypress that did not come from your fingers. So this checks rather than promises. ⇧⌘R is not in that group: it uses a shortcut Figma already ships, so it works either way.
